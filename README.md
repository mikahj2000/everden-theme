# Everden theme

A Shopify theme for Everden's red light knee wrap. The layout follows the section order of swishhairco.com (used with the owner's permission), in the Ember & Stone palette with Fraunces and Inter fonts.

## Connect it to Shopify

1. In Shopify admin, go to **Online Store → Themes**.
2. Under **Theme library**, click **Add theme → Connect from GitHub**.
3. Pick this repo (`everden-theme`) and the `main` branch.
4. Click **Customize** to preview it, then **Publish** when it's ready.

Shopify keeps the theme in sync with this branch. Changes pushed here show up in the theme, and changes made in the theme editor are committed back here.

## Pages

**Homepage:** announcement bar, hero, scrolling phrases, 3 numbered steps, 6 feature cards, numbers band, comparison table, customer reviews (hidden until real reviews are added), guarantee, FAQ, email sign-up, footer.

**Product page:** gallery, title, checklist, price, Single / 2-pack picker, add to cart, trust row, Specs / How to use / Shipping / Safety tabs, close-up, 3-step routine, numbers band, in the box, comparison, reviews, guarantee, FAQ, safety note, email sign-up.

Also included: cart, collection, search, contact, 404, blog, article and password pages.

## Before launch

- **Photos.** Striped boxes marked "Photo placeholder" describe the shot that belongs there. Add images in the theme editor. Product photos come from the product itself in Shopify.
- **Product.** Set the price to $89, and add a 2-pack variant at $149 with a compare-at price of $178 so the picker shows "You save $29".
- **Hero and buttons** point to the product by its handle. If you rename the product's URL handle, pick the product again in the Hero, Header and Guarantee settings.
- **Refund policy.** Set it in **Settings → Policies**. The guarantee section links to it.
- **Confirm with the supplier:** heat (max temperature, auto-shutoff), LED count, strap size, what's in the box, and FDA status. The copy mentions a warmth setting and avoids an LED count until those are confirmed.

## Claims rules

The copy avoids "cures," "treats," "pain relief," "FDA approved," "clinically proven" and "avoid surgery," and it has no made-up reviews or stats. Keep it that way when editing.
